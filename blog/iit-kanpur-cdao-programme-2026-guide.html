IIT Kanpur CDAO Programme — Overview

The Chief Data & AI Officer (CDAO) Programme by IIT Kanpur targets professionals preparing for Chief Data Officer (CDO), Chief AI Officer (CAIO) or VP Data & Analytics roles. IIT Kanpur is consistently ranked in India's top 5 IITs (NIRF #4 Engineering) with strong data science and AI research credentials. The programme gives IIT Kanpur alumni status.

What a CDAO Needs to Know

  • Data strategy — enterprise data architecture, data governance frameworks
  • AI governance — model risk management, responsible AI, bias/fairness
  • Analytics at scale — from insights to enterprise data products
  • CDO/CAIO board communication — articulating data ROI to non-technical executives
  • Data privacy and compliance — DPDP Act, GDPR implications for Indian organisations
  • Building data teams — data engineers, scientists, analysts, and ML engineers
  • AI strategy — build vs buy, make vs partner for AI capabilities

Who Should Do the IIT Kanpur CDAO Programme?

The ideal candidate: a data science lead, analytics manager, or head of data with 8-12 years of experience, currently at Director/VP level or targeting those roles. Professionals who have built strong technical expertise but need the executive leadership frameworks and IIT institutional credibility to operate at board level.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IIT Kanpur CDAO programme?

The Chief Data & AI Officer Programme by IIT Kanpur is a senior leadership programme for data and AI professionals targeting CDO, CAIO or VP Data roles. Covers data strategy, AI governance, enterprise analytics and executive communication. Gives IIT Kanpur alumni status.

What is the difference between CDO and CTO?

CDO (Chief Data Officer) owns data strategy, data governance, analytics and AI capability. CTO (Chief Technology Officer) owns technology architecture, engineering, infrastructure and product technology. In some organisations these roles overlap; in others they are distinct. IIT Kanpur CDAO is for data/AI leadership; IIT Bombay CTO is for engineering/technology leadership.

Is IIT Kanpur good for data science?

Yes — IIT Kanpur has strong research in machine learning, data science and AI. It is ranked #4 in India by NIRF (Engineering) and has produced significant research in these domains. The CDAO programme leverages this institutional strength.
