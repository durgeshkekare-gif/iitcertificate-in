IIT Roorkee Product Management Certificate — What It Is

IIT Roorkee's E&ICT Academy offers a Product Management certificate programme for working professionals. The programme is designed for engineers, data scientists, and IT professionals who want to transition into product management or formalise their PM credentials with an IIT institutional brand. Duration: approximately 6-9 months. Online with IIT Roorkee campus module. IIT Roorkee is NIRF #6 overall — one of India's most respected technical institutions.

What You Learn

  • Product discovery — user research, problem definition, opportunity sizing
  • Product strategy — vision, roadmap, prioritisation frameworks (RICE, ICE scoring)
  • Agile product development — sprint planning, backlog management, working with engineering
  • Metrics and analytics — North Star metric, funnel analysis, A/B testing
  • Go-to-market — product launch strategy, pricing, positioning
  • Stakeholder management — working with engineering, design, business, and leadership
  • Case studies — real Indian product case studies (Swiggy, Zepto, PhonePe, Nykaa)

Who Should Apply

  • Software engineers with 2-5 years experience targeting PM roles
  • Data scientists / analysts who want to move into product analytics or PM
  • IT project managers transitioning to product ownership at tech companies
  • Founders or aspiring entrepreneurs wanting structured product frameworks
  • Business analysts at tech companies targeting APM (Associate Product Manager) roles

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIT Roorkee Product Management certificate good?

Yes — IIT Roorkee is NIRF #6 and the E&ICT Academy PM programme gives IIT Roorkee alumni status with a structured PM curriculum. For engineers transitioning to PM, the IIT brand significantly improves shortlisting at tech companies and startups. At ~₹1.5-2L for 6-9 months, it is competitive with ISB's PM programme (~₹2.5-3L) at lower cost.

Can non-engineers do IIT Roorkee Product Management programme?

Yes — while IIT Roorkee PM programme is popular with engineers, business analysts and data professionals from non-engineering backgrounds are eligible. The programme benefits anyone with a technical or analytical background. Pure non-technical candidates (humanities background, sales) would find the curriculum technically demanding — verify prerequisites before applying.

IIT Roorkee PM or ISB PM — which is better?

Both are strong. ISB has stronger startup and venture capital network (Hyderabad ecosystem). IIT Roorkee has stronger engineering alumni network and IIT brand recognition at tech companies. Fee: IIT Roorkee is typically ₹1.5-2L vs ISB ₹2.5-3L. For candidates targeting product at large tech companies: IIT brand may edge ISB. For startup ecosystem and VC connections: ISB wins.
